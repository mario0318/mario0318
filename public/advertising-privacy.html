Advertising privacy and choices

Advertising is optional. This website works with advertising off.

Before you enable advertising

The advertising component does not contact Google until you choose Enable Google advertising. It stores only an on/off preference in this browser for this website. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control keep the component off, even if a previous preference allowed ads.

When advertising is enabled

Google AdSense and its advertising partners receive network information such as your IP address and browser information and may use cookies or device identifiers. Google can infer an approximate location from your IP address. Google receives information associated with the public page on which the advertising code runs. The component does not send account credentials, map coordinates or call searches as custom advertising events. Advertising is excluded from pages with unrecognised query parameters.

Google's certified consent-management message collects applicable European consent choices, and its US state regulations message provides applicable opt-out choices. Ad requests wait for the consent framework responses and are not made if the required choices are unavailable or refused. This component requests non-personalized ads; those ads can still use storage for purposes such as measurement and fraud prevention.

Change your mind

Use Advertising settings below or beside a placement. Keep advertising off saves your preference and reloads the page to stop further advertising requests. Google privacy choices reopens Google's consent controls after its service has loaded. Clearing this website's browser storage resets the local preference. Preferences are separate for each website.

See how Google uses information for advertising and Google's privacy policy for its processing and retention practices. Website hosting separately processes normal network-request logs. Existing product privacy notices continue to describe account, content, payment and other site features.

Availability and contact

An enabled preference does not guarantee an ad. AdSense approval, inventory, browser restrictions and your consent choices can leave a placement empty. Questions can be sent through the website's existing contact channel.
